Essential Safety Rules

Key principles to avoid mistakes, stay on free tiers, and keep data secure.

🔑 1. API Keys are Passwords

Treat keys like your bank PIN. Never share them in public links, code repositories, or chat screenshots.

🔒 2. Read Permissions

Plugs ask to read or write to Gmail, Asana, or Calendar. Always verify what access is granted before clicking Allow.

🎯 3. Tell AI Not to Invent

Always instruct AI models: "Never fabricate numbers or credentials. State clearly when information is missing."

🧪 4. Test First, Publish Last

Keep n8n automations inactive while building. Use safe test email accounts before turning live automation on.

⏹️ 5. Turn Off Live Triggers

Triggers checking inboxes or webhooks on a schedule consume credits. Switch them off when you finish testing.

💳 6. Skip Payment Screens

Start with free tiers and free credit allowances. Do not approve payment screens while learning.
