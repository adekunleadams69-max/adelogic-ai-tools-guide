Who to call, in order

Picked for braiding hair, edge control, hair care and nail basics. We already have a resale certificate, which distributors usually ask for.

Ask every supplier these 6 questions

Write the answers in the call log below.

  1. What do you need to open an account? Resale certificate, EIN, business license, or a salon license?
  2. What is the minimum for a first order, and for reorders?
  3. Do you ship to Pflugerville, TX? What does shipping cost and how long does it take?
  4. What are your 10 best-selling braiding hair and edge control items right now?
  5. Can we return or exchange items that do not sell?
  6. Do you offer payment terms after a few orders?

Phone setup (Spectrum)

  • Open the My Spectrum app or the voice portal at spectrumbusiness.net.
  • Find Call Guard. Business lines start on medium. Set it to the strictest level.
  • Check that call forwarding is turned on in the account. Do not set a forwarding code yet.
  • Ask Spectrum support for the correct code for forwarding unanswered calls.

Missed call count, 14 days
